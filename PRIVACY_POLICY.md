# Fitness Rest Lock Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Fitness Rest Lock is a local workout rest timer and app-locking tool. The app is designed to keep workout settings and session state on your device.

## Data We Process Locally

Fitness Rest Lock stores the following information on your device:

- Selected rest duration.
- Apps you allow during a lock period.
- Current workout session state, including timer end time and completed set count for the active session.

Fitness Rest Lock also queries launchable apps installed on your device so you can choose which apps stay allowed during a lock period.

## Accessibility Permission

Fitness Rest Lock uses Android Accessibility only for app-locking. When your rest timer reaches 0:00 and the app enters the decision lock, the Accessibility service detects the foreground app package name. If that app is not allowed, Fitness Rest Lock sends you back to the Android home screen and opens the workout decision screen.

Fitness Rest Lock does not use Accessibility to read messages, passwords, form text, notifications, or screen content for advertising or analytics.

## Advertising

Fitness Rest Lock uses Google AdMob rewarded ads. When you choose to watch an ad to support the creator, Google AdMob may process advertising-related data such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic data according to Google's policies.

Google's privacy policy is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Collection And Sharing

Fitness Rest Lock itself does not sell personal data and does not run its own remote user database. The app has no account system and stores no workout history in this MVP.

## Network Use

Fitness Rest Lock's core workout timer and app-locking features are designed to work locally. Network access may be used by Google AdMob to load rewarded ads when you choose to watch an ad.

## Data Deletion

You can delete the app's local data by uninstalling Fitness Rest Lock or by clearing the app's storage from Android system settings.
